## Optimization and English presentation

The five initial imported PNGs are 1774 × 887. Metadata-free WebP copies are 1440 × 720, quality 86/effort 6, using the existing Sharp dependency. They preserve the full original composition at exactly 2:1; no stretching or cropping is applied. Individual files are approximately 49–83 KB rather than 1.5–1.7 MB. Racker remains its unchanged original 1794 × 876 image, displayed contained in the shared 2:1 frame.

English pages do not reuse the Turkish marketing headlines of the imported PNGs. Their `*-en.webp` counterparts use deterministic SVG text and Sharp compositing, with accurate concise English descriptions beside the **complete existing approved screenshot**. No AI image generation, invented interface or fake English application UI is used. The label “Recorded original interface · Turkish” makes the native interface language explicit; “Promotional composition” identifies the composite. Lina says “Development paused”, Kutlumi “Unreleased personal project”. Output is 1440 × 720, approximately 17–36 KB. Original screenshots remain unchanged and are also available separately in galleries.

`src/data/covers.ts` is a typed, per-project/per-language map separate from `src/data/project-images.ts`. The existing screenshot inventory remains the source of truth for galleries, recorded comparisons and image navigators. Projects absent from the promotional map intentionally use an approved original image or simple typography; the excluded third-party face is never a fallback.

## Verification

Before/after SHA-256 values were compared for every imported source PNG. Racker's PNG and existing WebP were compared after raw pixel decoding and were identical. Every output was visually checked and is standard RIFF WebP with no EXIF/XMP/ICC chunks. The local review manifest records source and output dimensions, byte sizes and SHA-256 values; no confidential project source is included in this document.

These artwork checks do not establish ownership of third-party broadcast imagery, AI model performance, real-time processing throughput, or physical-device acceptance. The excluded Deepfake files require explicit verified publication permission before any future import.
